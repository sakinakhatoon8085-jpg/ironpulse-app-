Chest

1. Flat Dumbbell / Barbell Press

Push Motion • Mid Chest
Target: Mid Pectorals
Sets: 3-4 Sets
Reps: 8-12 Reps
Rest: 75 Sec

Form: Shoulder blades peeche lock rakhein. Dumbbells ko slow control ke saath press karein.

⚠️ Galti: Kohani ko 90° par mat failayein; 45° angle par rakhein.
Chest & Triceps

2. Standard Push-ups

Bodyweight Push
Target: Chest & Core
Sets: 3 Sets
Reps: 12-15 Reps
Rest: 60 Sec

Form: Body seedhi rakhein, chest zameen se 1 inch upar tak le jayein.

⚠️ Galti: Kamar ko zameen par jhulne na dein.
Back (Lats)

3. Lat Pulldown / Pull-ups

Vertical Pull • V-Taper
Target: Upper Lats
Sets: 3-4 Sets
Reps: 10-12 Reps
Rest: 75 Sec

Form: Bar ko collarbone ki taraf pull karein aur elbows ko peeche squeeze karein.

⚠️ Galti: Momentum se zyada peeche mat jhukiye.
Back (Thickness)

4. Bent-Over Row

Horizontal Row
Target: Mid Back
Sets: 3 Sets
Reps: 8-12 Reps
Rest: 75 Sec

Form: Kamar 45° bend rakhein aur weight naabhi (navel) ki taraf pull karein.

⚠️ Galti: Peeth ko bilkul round na karein.
Legs (Quads & Glutes)

5. Squats

Lower Body Compound
Target: Quads & Hips
Sets: 4 Sets
Reps: 8-12 Reps
Rest: 90 Sec

Form: Pair shoulder-width kholiye aur thighs zameen ke parallel laayein.

⚠️ Galti: Ghutno ko andar mat jhulne do; heels par balance rakho.
Legs (Hamstrings)

6. Romanian Deadlift (RDL)

Hip Hinge
Target: Hamstrings
Sets: 3 Sets
Reps: 10-12 Reps
Rest: 75 Sec

Form: Hips ko peeche push karein aur hamstrings mein stretch mehsoos karein.

⚠️ Galti: Ghutne poora bend nahi karne; yeh hinge hai.
Shoulders

7. Overhead Shoulder Press

Overhead Push
Target: Front & Side Delts
Sets: 3-4 Sets
Reps: 8-12 Reps
Rest: 75 Sec

Form: Kaan ke barabar se seedhe sir ke upar press karein.

⚠️ Galti: Kamar ko piche mat jhukayein.
Arms (Biceps)

8. Dumbbell Bicep Curls

Arm Curl
Target: Biceps Peak
Sets: 3 Sets
Reps: 10-14 Reps
Rest: 60 Sec

Form: Elbows ko kamar ke paas fix rakhein aur upar tight squeeze karein.

⚠️ Galti: Body ko swing mat karein.
Core

9. Forearm Plank

Core Tension
Target: Abs & Deep Core
Sets: 3 Sets
Hold: 45-60 Sec
Rest: 60 Sec

Form: Body seedhi aur tight squeeze rakhein.

⚠️ Galti: Hips ko neeche mat dabayein.
